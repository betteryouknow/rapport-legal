Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Social Skills Coach is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. In short: we don't require an account, we don't sell your data, and we don't use it to train models.

What we handle

To provide the app's core feature, the following is handled:

  • User Content (Other User Content) — not linked to you; not used for tracking; purpose: App Functionality.
  • User Content (Audio Data) — not linked to you; not used for tracking; purpose: App Functionality.
Practice messages you type — and, if you use voice practice, the audio you record — are sent to the backend LLM to generate the reply and feedback, then not retained. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Third-party processing

When you use an AI feature, your input is sent securely to our processing service (and its AI provider) solely to produce your result. It is processed in the request and not retained, and is never used to train models.

Your choices

You control what you submit. On-device data can be deleted at any time from within the app or by removing the app.

Children

Social Skills Coach is not directed to children under 13.
